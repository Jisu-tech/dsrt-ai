02 / THE ENTERPRISE PROPOSITION

Your AI environment already exists. We work with it.

Organizations have already invested in AI providers, applications, internal systems and operational workflows.

DSRT AI approaches enterprise deployment with that existing environment in mind.

The objective is to identify where LLM Amplification can add measurable economic value without requiring an organization to unnecessarily replace the systems it already depends on.

PRINCIPLE / 01

Work with existing investments

Assess the current AI environment before recommending changes.

Enterprise teams operate established provider agreements, API infrastructure, internal applications and governance controls. Technical discovery begins with those commitments rather than assuming a greenfield replacement.

PRINCIPLE / 02

Focus on economic improvement

Identify opportunities to improve the efficiency of AI expenditure.

Recurring model usage across internal operations and product workflows generates compounding compute expenditure. Engagements target specific workloads where amplification can improve cost-efficiency while preserving output standards.

PRINCIPLE / 03

Establish measurable outcomes

Define a baseline and evaluate the results of the agreed deployment.

Commercial value requires objective verification. Both organizations agree on baseline metrics, comparable workload parameters, output quality thresholds and net financial calculation methods prior to deployment.

07 / MEASUREMENT AND VALIDATION

Economic improvement must be demonstrated.

A lower AI bill is not sufficient if output quality, reliability or operational performance deteriorates.

Enterprise deployments should establish a baseline and evaluate the results using agreed measurements across comparable workloads, equivalent quality requirements and an agreed measurement period.

MEASUREMENT 01

Expenditure

Evaluate relevant AI provider expenditure before and after the deployment.

MEASUREMENT 02

Token consumption

Measure relevant input and output token consumption for comparable workloads.

MEASUREMENT 03

Output quality

Evaluate whether the resulting outputs meet the organization's established quality requirements.

MEASUREMENT 04

Reliability

Assess consistency, factual accuracy and task completion against agreed evaluation criteria.

MEASUREMENT 05

Processing overhead

Account for additional processing, amplification overhead and other relevant operational costs.

MEASUREMENT 06

Net economic impact

Compare the overall cost of the agreed deployment with the baseline.
