Dsrt AI · Manifesto

We force AI to work harder, think smarter, and cost less — empowering individuals and enterprises to do more with every model, every token, and every dollar.

There is so much left to build.

Build with DSRT AI because we understand what hurts your enterprise's profits.

01 · Our principle

More value from every AI dollar.

Businesses should get more value from every dollar spent on AI. Whether it's reducing unnecessary AI costs, improving the quality of everyday work, or making advanced AI capabilities more accessible, Dsrt aims to help individuals and enterprises do more with AI while spending less.
  • Spend only where quality demands it
  • Measure every token, not just every invoice
  • Make premium reasoning reachable on everyday budgets
Image Placeholderassets/images/sections/principle.png
02 · Intelligence at your budget

Route every task to the cheapest path that still passes.

Dsrt AI amplifies the models you already use. Each task is sent to the least expensive inference path that still meets your quality threshold, then verified with measured quality, cost and completion data — so you can see exactly where every token and every dollar goes.
  • Quality threshold set by you, enforced by Dsrt
  • Hybrid routing across providers and model tiers
  • Every run returns cost, quality and completion metrics
Image Placeholderassets/images/sections/intelligence.png
03 · Verified, not assumed

Proof travels with every answer.

Cheaper is only better if it still passes. Dsrt AI pairs every amplified output with a raw baseline, flags unsupported claims and speculative numbers, and reports the amplification overhead alongside the savings — so the economics are established by measurement, not by promise.
  • Side-by-side raw vs amplified comparison
  • Traceable calculations and labelled assumptions
  • Overhead included in every cost figure
