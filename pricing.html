Clear pricing. Clear subscription terms.

Everything you need to understand before you subscribe. No hidden conditions.

Billing frequency
All subscriptions are billed monthly. No annual billing option is currently offered.
Currency
All prices are displayed and charged in USD.
Founding Partner pricing
Displayed founding prices form part of the Founding Access offer. Eligibility, duration and renewal terms are confirmed at checkout.
Team billing
The Team subscription is charged per seat per month, with a minimum purchase of three seats.
Enterprise billing
Enterprise pricing is determined through an individual commercial agreement based on deployment scope.
External provider charges
Where external AI providers charge separately, those charges remain subject to their own terms.
Taxes
Applicable taxes are calculated at checkout according to your billing location.
Renewal & cancellation
Subscriptions renew monthly. You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
Usage limits
Included usage, fair-use policies and any additional charges are disclosed before purchase. No "unlimited" claim is made.

Frequently asked questions

The answers to the questions customers ask most often before subscribing.

DSRT AI is an AI cost-efficiency product that uses in-house LLM Amplification technology to help businesses get more value from their AI expenditure.
Work is designed for individuals and professionals. Team is designed for startups and organizations with multiple users. Enterprise is intended for larger deployments with custom requirements.
Founding Partner pricing is the discounted subscription pricing presented during the DSRT AI Founding Access offer. The planned standard prices are reference prices that are not currently active.
Work is $9.99 per month under Founding Partner pricing. The planned standard price is $19.99 per month.
Team is $12 per seat per month under Founding Partner pricing, with a minimum of three seats. For three seats, that is $36 per month under founding pricing, compared to a planned standard equivalent of $60 per month.
No. The Team subscription has a minimum purchase requirement of three seats. An individual should consider the Work subscription instead.
The monthly subscription total is calculated by multiplying the number of subscribed seats by $12 under Founding Partner pricing. Use the seat calculator on the Team card to estimate your total.
Enterprise pricing is custom and depends on deployment size, governance and integration requirements. Use the "Contact Enterprise Team" button to start a discussion.
BYOK is an optional configuration capability. It is not the primary subscription requirement. The applicable provider and billing arrangements depend on the selected product configuration.
No universal savings guarantee is implied. Actual results depend on your workload and measured performance. The 40% example shown in the economic-value calculator is illustrative unless supported by a specific verified customer result.
Subscription upgrades, downgrades and seat changes follow the rules implemented in the subscription system. Contact support or manage your subscription from your account. Founder price eligibility when changing plans is confirmed in your account terms.
When you cancel, access terminates at the end of your current billing period. Outstanding payments remain due. The refund policy is disclosed at checkout and is subject to the applicable subscription terms.
