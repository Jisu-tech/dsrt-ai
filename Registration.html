A Letter From The CEO

Letter from CEO
Jisu Mondal to
DSRT AI Partners.

Author
Jisu Mondal
Role
Chief Executive Officer, DSRT AI
Audience
Founding Partners · Early Access Applicants

Dear DSRT AI Partners,

Every meaningful organization is built on more than technology, ambition, or individual achievements. It is built on people who share a vision, believe in a common purpose, and are willing to move forward together.

As we continue building DSRT AI, I want to take a moment to express my sincere appreciation to each of you who has chosen to be part of this journey.

What we are building is ambitious. Our vision extends beyond developing a product or introducing another technology into the market. We are working towards creating something meaningful — an intelligence infrastructure that can empower organizations, expand possibilities, and make advanced AI more accessible, efficient, and useful.

But a vision of this scale cannot be achieved by one person or one team alone.

It requires trust, collaboration, shared responsibility, and, above all, a belief that we are working towards something bigger than ourselves.

I want DSRT AI to be an organization where every partner feels that their contribution matters, their voice is respected, and their growth is connected to the growth of the organization. Titles and positions may define responsibilities, but they should never create distance between the people working towards the same objective.

We may have different responsibilities, perspectives, experiences, and strengths, but we share one direction. Our differences should make us stronger, our challenges should bring us closer, and our achievements should belong to all of us.

There will undoubtedly be difficult days ahead. There will be decisions to make, obstacles to overcome, and moments that demand patience and perseverance. We should approach those moments with understanding rather than division, responsibility rather than blame, and cooperation rather than competition.

I believe that the strength of DSRT AI will not be measured only by the technology we develop or the milestones we achieve, but also by the culture we build along the way.

Let us build an environment founded on integrity, mutual respect, transparency, accountability, and unity. Let us celebrate one another's progress, support one another through difficulties, and remain committed to the standards we set for ourselves.

I do not want us to see DSRT AI merely as an organization we work with. I want us to see it as something we are collectively building, shaping, and taking forward — a shared responsibility and a shared opportunity.

Our journey has only begun, and there is much ahead of us. I look forward to what we can accomplish together, not simply as partners working under one name, but as people united by one vision and one purpose.

We build together. We grow together. We move forward together.

Thank you for your trust, dedication, and belief in DSRT AI.

Jisu Mondal
Chief Executive Officer · DSRT AI
